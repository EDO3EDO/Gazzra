Returns & Exchanges

@if (activeId === 'returns-1') {
We accept returns of unworn, unwashed items with original tags intact within 30 days of delivery. Refunds are issued to the original payment method, minus a small return shipping fee. Final sale items are not eligible for return.
}
@if (activeId === 'returns-2') {
Gift recipients can process an exchange or receive store credit without notifying the original purchaser. Simply visit our Returns Portal and select the "Returning a Gift" option, then enter the order number provided on the gift receipt.

Care Instructions

@if (activeId === 'care-1') {
For our organic cotton essentials, we recommend machine washing on a gentle, cold cycle with similar colors using a mild, baby-safe detergent. Tumble dry on low or lay flat to dry to preserve the softness and prevent shrinkage.
}
@if (activeId === 'care-2') {
Delicate knits should be hand-washed in cool water and laid flat to dry to maintain their shape. Linen pieces can be machine washed on delicate/cold, but should always be air-dried. A cool iron may be used on linen if desired.
